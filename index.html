📖 Teen Patti Rules & Hand Hierarchy

In Teen Patti (Flash/Flush), hands consist of 3 cards. Ranked from highest to lowest:

#1 HIGHEST
Trail / Trio / Set (Three of a Kind)
Three cards of the exact same rank. A-A-A is the highest possible hand, followed by K-K-K, down to 2-2-2 (lowest).
A♠ A♥ A♦ Ex: A-A-A beats K-K-K
#2 PURE
Pure Sequence / Straight Flush (Pakki Round)
Three consecutive cards of the same suit. In traditional Teen Patti, A-2-3 is highest, followed by A-K-Q, K-Q-J, down to 4-3-2.
A♥ 2♥ 3♥ Ex: A-2-3♥ or A-K-Q♠
#3 STRAIGHT
Sequence / Straight (Normal Round)
Three consecutive cards of different suits. A-2-3 is highest, followed by A-K-Q, K-Q-J, down to 4-3-2.
A♠ 2♥ 3♣ Ex: A-2-3 mixed suits
#4 COLOR
Color / Flush (Rang)
Three cards of the same suit, not in sequence. If two players have a color, the highest individual card wins (e.g. A-K-J beats A-K-10).
A♦ K♦ 9♦ Ex: All Diamonds
#5 PAIR
Pair (Double)
Two cards of the same rank + one kicker card. Higher pair wins (A-A-x beats K-K-x). If pairs are equal, the higher third card (kicker) decides.
K♠ K♣ 9♥ Ex: Pair of Kings
#6 HIGH
High Card
Three mismatched cards of different ranks and suits. Hands are ranked card by card from highest: A > K > Q > J > 10 ... > 2.
A♠ J♥ 4♣ Ex: Ace High
🪙 1. Boot / Ante

At the start of every hand, the Boot amount (default 5 chips) is automatically collected from each player into the Main Pot before any cards are dealt. If a player had 0 chips at hand start or was added mid-hand, their boot ante is automatically collected into the pot as soon as they top up / add bankroll.

👁️ 2. Blind vs. Seen (1 : 2 Ratio)

Blind players pay 1x the Blind stake (which is ½ of the Chaal stake). Seen players pay 2x the Blind stake (full Chaal stake).
Seeing cards is permanent: Once a player clicks "SEE CARDS", they remain Seen for the rest of that hand.

📈 3. Monotonic Call Ratchet & Raises

The table stake never decreases; it only moves upwards. Any raise must be strictly greater than the current required call. Once raised, all subsequent players must match at least the new raised stake.

🔥 4. All-In & 2-Button Lock

Any active player can click ALL IN to push their entire bankroll into the pot.
Once initiated, all subsequent players are locked into strictly two choices: PACK (Fold) or CALL ALL-IN.
Per-Round Matching: Opponents match the All-In bet placed in the active round.
Low Bankroll / Buyout: If an opponent does not have enough chips to match the call, the button shows LOW CHIPS, and they can either PACK or ADD BANKROLL to cover the shortage.

🏁 5. Round Limit & Final Showdown

Hands run for a maximum of 3 rounds. Showdown is available at the end of Round 3 or when ≤ 2 active players remain.
When an All-In occurs, that round is strictly the final round—once all active players have responded, the hand immediately concludes with the Showdown.
If all players except one fold, the remaining player automatically wins the entire pot.

🔄 6. Dealer & Turn Rotation

When a player wins the pot, the Dealer button moves to the winner, and the next hand begins with the player to the immediate left (clockwise) of the winner.

💰 7. Rebuys & Top-Ups

Players can add chips to their bankroll at any time (minimum +100 chips) by clicking their Bankroll box on their table pod.
